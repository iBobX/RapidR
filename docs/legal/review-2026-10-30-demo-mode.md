No NOTICE needed in the video. Trademarks: "Qwen3-TTS", "GitHub Sponsors" and "Buy Me a Coffee" as plain text naming the model or the service are nominative use; no logos on the cards (`first-app.json`, `design/brand/logo/rapidr-lockup-dark.svg` and `card-bg.svg` are RapidR's own, plain shapes and gradients).
- **EU AI Act Art. 50** (applies from 2 Aug 2026 as the Act is written; later amendments not checked): the marking duty (50(2)) is the generator's provider's (Qwen, MLX-Serve), not Robert's as a user; the disclosure duty (50(4)) is for deep fakes, content resembling real people or events that could pass as authentic. An invented narrator voice over a screen recording is not one, so the credit is voluntary, kept for transparency. No US federal rule found for it; state deepfake laws target impersonation and elections (not checked state by state).
- **Narration server:** key from the Keychain at run time, in memory only, in no log or error message (`narration.mjs:28-35,59,73`); the key is only in the Authorization header, and the output lines show clip text, never the key. ## Before RTutor goes public (Preview 6)

1. Only the app's own windows, through the kernel; never the system's mouse or keyboard, other apps, a password or system dialog (the 2026-10-29 `app-eyes` conditions apply).
2. A pointer you can always see (the overlay's ghost cursor) and a visible "tour playing" marker; reduce-motion turns the movement off.
3. The user stays in charge: Pause, Stop, Skip, take over; Esc exits; their own click or key ends the automation; never auto-start; no focus trap.
4. A different code path from the test hook: no environment variable, no `inbox/` or `app/in-N.json` file channel, in-process only; an allowlist of actions (no `Micro::Do` of any Studio command; no click on Send, Pay or Delete without the user's own).
5. Accessibility: captions always, keyboard-only and screen-reader route (targets by accessible name), no speech over a running screen reader, no flashing (`docs/speech-and-tutorial-plan.md`, WCAG 2.2).
6. Nothing recorded or sent: tours and RCaptions run locally, mask inputs, log no typed text. If RapidR ever ships a voice generator, RapidR becomes the Art. 50(2) *provider* (machine-readable marking) and the model licence and voice provenance need their own review; exporting video uses royalty-free codecs only.
7. Name search for `RTutor` and `RCaptions`, then a lawyer-agent review, a manual page, an example and the AI facet before announcing.
