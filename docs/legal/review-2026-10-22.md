# Legal review, 2026-10-22: this week's additions

This review was run by RapidR's "lawyer agent", a standing rule since 2026-10-22: every new dependency, protocol, format, asset, name or channel is reviewed before it lands. It is a careful risk review with sources, **not legal advice**.
- **Searches:** run on 2026-10-22. The USPTO was searched by script; everything else on the web.
- **Not yet searched:** TMview and WIPO, which still need a manual look.
- **Status:** the fixes are tracked in the "Status" column, and the lead updates it.

## Decisions for Robert
1. **Signing:** sign only RapidR's own tools. Export runners stay unsigned or ad-hoc, and users sign their own apps. Recommended.
2. **Fonts:** approve OFL-1.1 for font files only. It already covers the shipped Noto Sans SC and KR.
3. **The Mac signer:** our own ad-hoc signer, not apple-codesign. Recommended.
4. **The name:** keep "RapidR Database Studio" as the full name, after the manual EU and WIPO search.
5. **Support money:** do the support accounts belong to Robert or to Ruta Internet? One-time support only, or recurring too?
6. **The company's country:** confirm it for tax advice.
7. **Optional:** a USPTO filing for "RapidR" (about $350 per class), which helps remove copycat store apps.
8. **The R3D family's new name.**
