# Legal review addendum, 2026-10-30: Studio in Spanish (the 60 `# legal` texts)

Lawyer-agent review of the Spanish (español neutro, tú) translation of the legal texts, for `ide/lang/es.po`. A careful risk review, **not legal advice** (US/EU practice; Uruguayan law not checked). Nothing was edited or committed; this file only lists what to change.

- **Compared against:** the English msgids the lawyer already approved (`docs/legal/review-2026-10-29-mcp.md`, `docs/legal/review-2026-10-29-app-eyes.md`, including the consent-dialog rewording, F2 and the "Wording to use" strings), and the side-by-side table in `docs/i18n/review-es-studio.md` §3.
- **Read:** all 60 `# legal` entries in `ide/lang/es.po` (`grep -c "^# legal"` = 60), the consent card in `crates/rapidr-studio/src/ai.rs:802-812`, and the key-store words in `crates/rapidr-ai/src/keys.rs`.
- **Test applied to each text:** (1) is every warning and condition of the English still there ("may cost money", "under 18", "only on this computer", "never sees your keys", "use test data", "may pass what it reads to the company that makes it, under that company's terms", "private data", "for adults"); (2) is any sentence a stronger promise than the English; (3) can it be misread in a less protective way; (4) neutral Spanish, tú.

## Overall verdict: OK AFTER CHANGES

The translation is faithful. All conditions above are present in every text that carries them, no warning is softened, and no promise is stronger than the English (every "nunca" / "Nunca ve" sits where the English has "never" / "It never sees", and the E-fix of the app-eyes addendum, "Studio's own tools never hand over…", is kept: "Las herramientas propias de Studio nunca entregan…"). The button and menu words match the pane: Permitir una vez, Permitir en esta sesión, Denegar, Permitir ediciones, Permitir todo, Restablecer token, Borrar actividad, Guardar clave, Actividad de aplicaciones de IA, Asistente de IA.

But **5 changes are required before Spanish ships** (R1 a misreadable sentence, R2 a cost understatement for Spanish-speaking readers, R3 and R4 English leaking into the consent card and the key sentences from Rust code, R5 an ambiguous subject in the per-run consent), plus 3 recommended.

## Checked and fine (no change)

- **Age / adults:** all five cloud texts keep "Cloud services are for adults / if you're under 18, ask a parent or teacher": `Los servicios en la nube son para personas adultas: si tienes menos de 18 años, pide a un padre, una madre o un docente…`; the AI-apps note keeps "Muchas aplicaciones de IA son solo para personas adultas: si tienes menos de 18 años, configura esto con un padre, una madre o un docente."
- **Costs and what is sent:** "may cost money" is present in all three places (`(lo cual puede costar dinero)`); "what its windows show … goes to that provider, under its privacy terms" is present in every provider text; DeepSeek keeps "en China".
- **Third-party terms:** `…puede pasar lo que lee a la empresa que la fabrica, bajo los términos de esa empresa` keeps "may" (`puede`) and "under that company's terms".
- **Keys:** every "never in a project / never in the code / your key isn't in it" is kept with the same strength (`nunca en un proyecto`, `nunca en el código`, `tu clave no está en ella`); "Your key is never shown or logged" is as absolute as the approved English, no more.
- **Local only:** `en esta computadora` is kept in the audit log text, the chat history text (`en la carpeta propia de Studio — nunca en el proyecto`), the clear-activity text and the server note (except R1).
- **Allow everything:** lead, "Ten en cuenta", "Sigues viendo" and the "Nunca" row (`Las herramientas propias de Studio nunca entregan…`, the already-corrected wording) are faithful; "what they click in it is real" and "only while you watch an AI app you trust" are kept; `Permitir todo no pasa a un proyecto nuevo` is kept.
- **"It never sees":** `Nunca ve` / `Tus claves de proveedores de IA o tus contraseñas: tu llavero las guarda.` equal to the English. As the app-eyes review notes, this row stays true only while `capture` refuses when Settings or a visible token is on screen (code condition B2, unchanged by the translation).
- **Secret check:** both plural forms keep "versions are often shared", "stays in the timeline", "take it out first, or save anyway if it isn't a real one".
- **Copyright line:** only "MIT licence" is translated; the licence name and holder stay.
- **Register:** tú throughout (`pega`, `elige`, `pide`, `ten en cuenta`); `computadora`, `presiona`, `docente`, `un padre, una madre` are neutral Latin-American Spanish. No voseo, no vosotros.

## Observations on the English (for the English owner, not blocking Spanish)

1. `A key ({hint}) is found in the {source}` with `{source}` = "an environment variable" renders "found in the an environment variable". Drop "the" in the English too.
2. The card says "click and type", but the same card gates looking at the program (app-eyes R2: "looking at the layout follows the same card"). The second sentence ("It sees whatever the program shows") covers it. If the English is changed to "look at and use" for parity with the pane question, the Spanish becomes `RAI quiere mirar y usar tu programa en ejecución.`
3. "Nothing you ask leaves this computer" (local model) is unhedged. If Settings lets a user type any server address for a "local" server, add "(a model on this computer)" or check that the address must be local.
