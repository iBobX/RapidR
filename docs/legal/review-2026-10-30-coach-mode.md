# Legal/wording review, 2026-10-30: Coach mode and "Keep RAI in Coach mode" (lane COACH-MODE)

Date: 2026-10-30. A careful wording review from the files themselves. **Not legal advice** (US/EU practice; no lawyer consulted).

**Scope.** English: `docs/manual/for-teachers.md` (Coach rows, sections, policy block, 2 Questions), `docs/manual/ai-assistant.md` ("Coach mode" to "What Coach promises"), `ide/aicoach.inc` and the Coach `TR$` texts in `ide/schoollock.inc`. Spanish: `docs/manual/es/for-teachers.md`, `docs/manual/es/ai-assistant.md`, the matching `ide/lang/es.po` entries. Facts checked in `crates/rapidr-studio/src/coach.rs`, `ai.rs` (`may_propose`, `coach`, `coach_refused`, `Handle::tools`, `Handle::call`, `app_call`), `ai_tools.rs`, `school_lock.rs`.

## Verdict: OK AFTER FIXES

The strong claim is true and code-enforced: in Coach the change tools (`propose_edit`, `write_file`, `create_project`, `layout`) are not offered (`ai.rs` `Handle::tools`, `may_propose`) and are refused if called (`Handle::call` -> `coach_refused`; `coach()` asks School Lock on every call). Insert links are off. `coach.rs` has no file write, no network call, no logging. But three texts say more than the code does (M1-M3), and the "stores nothing" answer is not true as written (M2).

## Must-fix

**M1. "Hints only, never the answer" is false: hint 4 shows the code.**
- `ide/schoollock.inc:263` (+ `ide/lang/es.po:5148-5149`); `docs/manual/for-teachers.md:122` and `es/for-teachers.md:137` (TOML comment). Also `crates/rapidr-cli/src/school_lock.rs:203` ("hints only": acceptable, optional).
- Risk: a teacher or parent reads "never the answer" and relies on it; the fourth hint is the answer, as text.
- EN `schoollock.inc:263`: "RAI stays in Coach mode in RapidR Studio on this computer: it gives hints one step at a time and doesn't change the student's files."
- ES: "RAI se mantiene en modo tutor en RapidR Studio en esta computadora: da pistas paso a paso y no cambia los archivos del estudiante."
- EN TOML comment: `# RAI on, in Coach mode only: hints, and it can't change the student's files`. ES: `# RAI activo, solo en modo tutor: pistas; no puede cambiar los archivos del estudiante`.

**M2. "Coach stores nothing / keeps nothing more than your chat" is not accurate.**
- `docs/manual/for-teachers.md:155`, `es/for-teachers.md:178`; `docs/manual/ai-assistant.md:441-442`, `es/ai-assistant.md:456-457`.
- Facts: Next Hint and Show the Code send visible chat messages "Give me the next hint." / "Show me the code." (`ide/aicoach.inc:482-490`), so the stored chat shows how many hints were asked. The audit log records "every request" (`ai-assistant.md:625-633`) with Studio's "hint N of 4" line. The chosen mode is saved (`aicoach.inc:325-327`, registry value `AIMode`). Coach adds no logging of its own (checked), but "keeps no record of who asked what or how many hints" reads as a privacy promise and is false. FERPA/GDPR-K-minded readers will rely on it.
- EN `for-teachers.md:155`: "**Does Coach mode store anything about students?** Coach adds nothing of its own: no names, no scores, no reports. RAI's usual records still apply: the chat and RAI's audit log stay on the computer and hold what the student asked, including each *Give me the next hint.* they pressed, and Studio remembers which mode was chosen. What is sent to the AI provider is the same kind of thing as in Build mode (the question, the chat, what RAI reads), plus Studio's own Coach instructions; **Tools > What Was Sent to the AI...** shows it. What a cloud provider does with it follows that provider's terms, which RapidR doesn't control. Cloud AI services are for adults ([Privacy and safety](../manual/ai-assistant.md#privacy-and-safety)): for students under 18, a teacher or the school makes the key, or a model on the computer is used."
- ES: "**¿El modo tutor guarda algo sobre los estudiantes?** El modo tutor no añade nada propio: ni nombres, ni puntajes, ni informes. Siguen los registros habituales de RAI: el chat y el registro de auditoría de RAI se quedan en la computadora y contienen lo que el estudiante preguntó, incluido cada *Dame la siguiente pista.* que pulsó, y Studio recuerda qué modo se eligió. Lo que se envía al proveedor de IA es del mismo tipo que en el modo construir (la pregunta, el chat, lo que RAI lee), más las instrucciones propias del modo tutor de Studio; **Herramientas > Qué se envió a la IA...** lo muestra. Lo que un proveedor en la nube hace con ello depende de las condiciones de ese proveedor, que RapidR no controla. Los servicios de IA en la nube son para adultos ([Privacidad y seguridad](../manual/es/ai-assistant.md#privacidad-y-seguridad)): para menores de 18, un docente o la escuela crea la clave, o se usa un modelo en la computadora." (Menu and anchor names checked against the Spanish page.)
- EN `ai-assistant.md:441`: "- Coach sends the same kinds of things as Build (your question, the chat, what RAI reads), plus Studio's own Coach instructions, and keeps the same: your chat and the audit log, and which mode you chose." ES: "- El modo tutor envía lo mismo que el modo construir (tu pregunta, el chat, lo que RAI lee), más las instrucciones propias del modo tutor, y guarda lo mismo: tu chat, el registro de auditoría y el modo que elegiste."

**M3. The "answers" Question presents prompt-level behaviour as a guarantee and reads as an assessment control.**
- `docs/manual/for-teachers.md:153`, `es/for-teachers.md:175`.
- Facts: file changes are blocked in code. Hint pacing ("code only after three hints", "No code" on hints 1-3) is only an instruction to the model (`coach.rs` `rung_line`, `instructions`); Studio does not filter a model's answer, and a model (small ones especially) may write more. The code is three Next Hint clicks away. Copy works, so "type in" understates. "AI apps (MCP) keeps those out of Studio" can be read as covering the AI websites just named.
- EN: "**Does Coach mode stop students from getting answers?** No. It encourages them to try first; it isn't a way to prevent copying or to assess work. In RapidR Studio, RAI can't change a student's files while it's kept in Coach mode (Studio refuses it in its code). RAI is told to give one hint at a time and to show code only as the fourth hint, which three clicks of Next Hint reach; Studio can't check every word a model writes, so a model may say more than asked. The student can type or copy that code in. Coach doesn't stop other AI websites or apps on the computer, or help from other people; *AI apps (MCP)* only stops AI apps from connecting to Studio."
- ES: "**¿El modo tutor impide que los estudiantes reciban respuestas?** No. Los anima a intentarlo primero; no sirve para impedir copias ni para evaluar trabajos. En RapidR Studio, RAI no puede cambiar los archivos de un estudiante mientras se mantiene en modo tutor (Studio lo rechaza en su código). A RAI se le indica dar una pista a la vez y mostrar código solo como cuarta pista, a la que se llega con tres clics en Siguiente pista; Studio no puede comprobar cada palabra que escribe un modelo, así que un modelo podría decir más de lo pedido. El estudiante puede escribir o copiar ese código. El modo tutor no impide otros sitios web o aplicaciones de IA en la computadora, ni la ayuda de otras personas; *Aplicaciones de IA (MCP)* solo impide que las aplicaciones de IA se conecten a Studio."

## Should-fix

**S1. "What Coach promises" and "never skips one".** `ai-assistant.md:427` + `es/ai-assistant.md:441` ("Lo que promete el modo tutor"); `ai-assistant.md:409-410` + `es:423-424`. Only the first bullet is a code-enforced promise; the rest (ladder, language, "No code") are instructions to a model. Rename: EN "How Coach works", ES "Cómo funciona el modo tutor"; split the first bullet as "What Studio enforces". EN for 409: "Asking in other words doesn't move you to the next hint: Studio keeps the count, and RAI is told which hint to write." ES: "Preguntar con otras palabras no te pasa a la siguiente pista: Studio lleva la cuenta y a RAI se le indica qué pista escribir."

**S2. "Without getting the answer" + no scope line where teachers decide.** `for-teachers.md:38` / `es:42`. Replace "without getting the answer" with "without being handed the whole answer at once" ("sin recibir toda la respuesta de golpe"). Add one sentence at the end of the section (`:50` / `es:55`): "Coach is a teaching aid inside RapidR Studio; it doesn't block AI websites or other programs (see *How strong is it?*)." ES: "El modo tutor es una ayuda para enseñar dentro de RapidR Studio; no bloquea sitios web de IA ni otros programas (mira *¿Qué tan fuerte es?*)."

**S3. "Type in" vs Copy.** `for-teachers.md:38`, `:153`; `es:42`, `:175` say "as text they type in themselves"; the UI has Copy and hint 4 says "type or copy" (`aicoach.inc:401`). Use "type or copy in" / "escriben o copian".

**S4. "The lock holds in Studio's code" can read as tamper-proof.** `for-teachers.md:50` / `es:55`. A per-account lock is "a classroom tool, not a wall" (same page, *How strong is it?*); in a browser it is "a reminder". Add "(only the computer's policy can't be removed by students; see *How strong is it?*)" and write "**Unlock Until Studio Closes** (with the password) lets Build be chosen again until Studio closes" ("con la contraseña").

**S5. Unprovable learning claim, stronger in Spanish.** `ai-assistant.md:382` "that's how it sticks" vs `es:395` "así se aprende de verdad". Use EN "writing the code yourself helps it stick", ES "escribir el código tú mismo ayuda a que se te quede".

**S6. "Look at your running program" omits the consent.** `ai-assistant.md:433-435` / `es:448-450`. `ai.rs` `app_call` asks the student once per run; the program's windows can show private data. Add "(after you allow it for that run)" / "(cuando lo permites en esa ejecución)".

**S7. Mark the legal-weight texts.** The project's convention (`docs/i18n/glossary-es.md` 1.11; `# legal` in `es.po`, `<!-- legal -->` in the manual) is missing on the Coach texts that say what is stored or sent: `es.po:5098-5099`, `5148-5149`; `es/for-teachers.md:42`, `:175`; `es/ai-assistant.md:456-457`. Add the marks so the Spanish gets the same check as the English.

- N2. Tone passes: no "easy", no shaming, no supervision wording ("Your teacher keeps RAI in Coach mode." only says who set it). `coach.rs` tells the model never to say anything is easy or obvious and to be kind when refusing more than the hint.
- N3. Third-party names: none in the checked Coach texts. The only hits are a code comment (`ide/schoollock.inc:753`, the older BASIC's sizes), not user-visible, and the pre-existing "GitHub" at `for-teachers.md:13`. - N6. On a computer where students share one account, chats and the audit log are shared too; worth one line in the teachers' page.
- N7. Verified as stated: tools not offered and refused in Coach (full and small-model modes); the lock is read on every call; `coach_only` must be a boolean (else the file fails closed, `school_lock.rs:384-394`); Insert links off; no new file write or network call in `coach.rs`.
