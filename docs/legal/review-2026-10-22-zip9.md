# Lawyer-agent review: zip 9 and typed-path (2026-10-22)

Lane X2 (Preview 4 security) upgraded `zip` 0.6.6 → 9.0.5 to clear RUSTSEC-2026-0367/0368/0369 (in `extract()`, which RapidR doesn't use outside a test). It brought one new crate, `typed-path` 0.12.4. Reviewed before merging, read-only, against the registry sources.

**Verdict: OK to merge.**

- **zip 9.0.5:** MIT (LICENSE © 2014 Mathijs van de Nes); no build script, no non-Rust sources, no other licence inside. Repository github.com/zip-rs/zip2, the continuation that publishes the crates.io `zip` crate. 9.0.5 not yanked.
- **typed-path 0.12.4:** MIT OR Apache-2.0; no build script, no dependencies, no bundled C. Its LICENSE-MIT has no copyright line: the notices credit the author named in its Cargo.toml.
- **What zip pulls in with RapidR's features** (default features off, `deflate-flate2-zlib-rs` only): crc32fast, flate2 + zlib-rs (Zlib), indexmap, memchr (Unlicense OR MIT), typed-path, and `nix` + `cfg_aliases` (MIT) on macOS/BSD. No GPL/LGPL/AGPL/MPL; no C or system libraries (bzip2, zstd, lzma and the rest are off).
- **Notices:** THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md lists typed-path and zip 9.0.5; `tools/third_party_notices.py --check` passes.
- **Checks:** `cargo deny --offline check` (all four) and `cargo audit --no-fetch` pass.
- **deny.toml / .cargo/audit.toml ignores** RUSTSEC-2026-0370/0371/0372 (zvariant, zbus_names, zvariant_derive): informational "unmaintained", no vulnerability, merged into zbus 6; Linux D-Bus only (screen-reader bridge, tray, keyring). Revisit when accesskit_unix, atspi or secret-service move to zbus 6.
- **At each release:** re-run `cargo deny` and `cargo audit` (zip patch releases are frequent).
